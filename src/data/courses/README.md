# Bundled course geometry

One JSON file per course (`CourseGeometry`), loaded on demand by the app (`seedLoaders.ts`).
Generated by the scripts in `GolfOS/lidar` and `GolfOS/features/platzdaten-methode` – do not edit by hand.

Golf geometry of the test courses from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), taken 2026-10-10 via the OSM API.
Son Quint: OSM has no holes there, so the 18 hole lines (yellow tee → green centre), greens and bunkers were added on 2026-10-10
from the official course guide 2026 (hole positions, par, Hcp, lengths) and the PNOA aerial image (IGN España, CC BY 4.0).
Son Vida: likewise from the official course guide 2026 (hole positions, par, Hcp, yellow lengths) and PNOA imagery.
Son Muntaner: OSM snapshot of 2026-10-11 (all 18 holes mapped there); stroke index of hole 10 corrected to 4 (scorecard 2026).
Alcanada, Canyamel, Son Térmens, Maioris, Pula, T Golf Puntiró: OSM snapshots of 2026-10-11 (all 18 holes mapped there).
Son Gual: OSM snapshot of 2026-10-11; hole 5 was tagged as a second hole 3 in OSM (corrected after the official scorecard).
greenSlopes and fairwaySlopes (Mallorca): LiDAR terrain model 2014, 2 m (Govern de les Illes Balears, IDEIB) – lidar/ib_install.py.
boundary: course outline from OSM (2026-10-11). fairwaySlopes: every 10 m along each hole [m from tee, cross slope %, height m] –
Hetzenhof: DGM1 Baden-Württemberg (1 m); Mallorca: LiDAR 2014 (2 m, IDEIB) – see the line above.
trees: woods/scrub from OSM (2026-10-11).
Coordinates are [lon, lat].
